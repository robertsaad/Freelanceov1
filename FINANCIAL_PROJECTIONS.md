## Revenue Model & Assumptions

### Subscription Plans

| Plan | Price | Annual Equivalent | Target Mix |
|------|-------|-------------------|------------|
| Monthly | $19.99/month | $239.88/year | 50% |
| Annual | $149.99/year | $149.99/year | 50% |
| **Blended Average** | **~$15/month** | **~$180/year** | **100%** |

### Revenue Assumptions

**Year 1:**
- Average revenue per user (ARPU): $15/month
- Mix: 50% monthly, 50% annual
- No premium features
- No price increases

**Year 2-3:**
- ARPU increases to $16-18/month
- Premium features add 10% revenue
- Mix shifts to 40% monthly, 60% annual
- 5% price increase in Year 3

**Year 4-5:**
- ARPU: $20/month
- Premium features: 15% of revenue
- Mix: 30% monthly, 70% annual
- 10% price increase in Year 4

### User Growth Assumptions

**Conversion Funnel:**
```
Visitors → Signups → Paid Subscribers

Year 1:
10,000 visitors/month → 500 signups (5%) → 60 paid (12% conversion)

Year 2:
50,000 visitors/month → 2,500 signups (5%) → 500 paid (20% conversion)

Year 3:
150,000 visitors/month → 7,500 signups (5%) → 1,875 paid (25% conversion)
```

**Churn Assumptions:**
- Monthly churn: 4% (industry standard for SaaS)
- Annual churn: 48% (inverse of retention)
- Target retention: 70% after 12 months
- Negative churn from upsells (Year 2+)

**Growth Drivers:**
1. Organic content marketing
2. Paid advertising
3. Referral program (15-20% of growth)
4. Word of mouth
5. Network effects

## Unit Economics

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Calculation Method: Historical Cohort**

**Assumptions:**
- Average monthly revenue per user: $15
- Gross margin: 87%
- Average customer lifetime: 24 months
- Monthly churn: 4%

**Formula:**
```
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Churn Rate
LTV = ($15 × 0.87) / 0.04
LTV = $13.05 / 0.04
LTV = $326.25
```

**Conservative Estimate: $360** (includes upsells)

**With Premium Features (Year 2+):**
```
ARPU increases to $18
Lifetime increases to 30 months

LTV = ($18 × 0.87) / 0.04
LTV = $15.66 / 0.04
LTV = $391.50
```

**Optimistic Estimate: $540** (Year 2+ with low churn)

### Payback Period

**Definition:** Time to recover CAC from customer revenue

**Formula:**
```
Payback Period = CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin)
```

**Year 1:**
```
Payback = $78 / ($15 × 0.87)
Payback = $78 / $13.05
Payback = 5.98 months
```

**Year 2-3:**
```
Payback = $35 / ($18 × 0.87)
Payback = $35 / $15.66
```
