## Unit Economics

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) - Lebanon

**Assumptions:**
- Average ARPU: $8.74/month
- Gross margin: 85%
- Average customer lifetime: 30 months (2.5 years)
- Monthly churn: 3% (lower due to crisis dependency)

**Calculation:**
```
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Churn Rate
LTV = ($8.74 × 0.85) / 0.03
LTV = $7.43 / 0.03
LTV = $247.67

Conservative estimate: $250
With upsells (Year 2+): $300
```

**Why Lower Than Global ($360-540):**
- Lower pricing ($9.99 vs $19.99)
- But also lower CAC
- Still excellent LTV:CAC ratio

```
Revenue per subscriber: $8.74

```

- Projected: Month 7 (374 subs, $3,269 MRR)

**Why Fast Break-Even:**

- Strong contribution margin (85%)
